# Changelog

What's new in each version of Mochi Companion. Mochi is still an early
preview, so things may change.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-19)

- **Mochi celebrates with you.** Tell Mochi you finished something, like "I
  finished my homework!", and it cheers for that exact thing. Then you can
  pick **Check my work**, **Take a break** (five minutes, shown in the status
  bar), or, on a light theme, **Switch to a dark theme**. Mochi notices this
  on your computer, with no AI, and doesn't celebrate when you're frustrated
  or just asking a question.
- **A "Make it yours" page.** Everything you can change, on one page: your
  name, how often Mochi speaks up, Learning mode, the brain, how long a
  focus session lasts, when late night starts, style notes for how Mochi
  sounds, and your own lines for each moment. Open it with **Mochi: Make it
  yours**, or from the **...** menu in Mochi's tab. It saves as you go.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-11)

The first preview.

- **Mochi's tab**, in the side bar on the right, with a cat whose mood shows
  in the status bar.
- **Chat with Mochi** about your code. Your conversation is kept for each
  project, so it's there when you come back.
- **Explaining errors and code**, from the lightbulb next to an error or
  **Explain this** on selected code. Learning mode, on by default, explains
  first, then offers a hint, and shows the fix only when you ask.
- **A brain of your choice:** GitHub Copilot (the free plan works), or your
  own Claude API key. **Mochi: Choose a brain** switches anytime.
- **Company while you code:** a greeting when you open VS Code, a gentle
  "Need a paw?" when an error sticks around, late-night company, a focus
  timer, and quiet mode, where Mochi naps in the status bar.
- **Switch to a dark theme**, with Undo.
- **Mochi: Set up a Python project** creates a virtual environment and asks
  before installing anything.
- **Asks before sharing your code**, names the AI it's going to, and never
  sends `.env` or key files. **Mochi: Forget everything** clears your chats
  and what Mochi remembers about you.
