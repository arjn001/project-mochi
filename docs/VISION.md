# Project Mochi: Vision

## In one sentence
A cozy AI companion that lives inside VS Code. It keeps you company, helps when
you're stuck, and does small things for you, so time in the editor feels
wonderful instead of overwhelming.

## Who it's for
- First, one person: a CS student who loves creative things, psychology, cats,
  and warm vintage aesthetics, and who finds coding overwhelming.
- Then anyone who finds coding lonely or intimidating.

## What it should feel like
Warm, present, and never in the way. Encouraging, never scary.

## What it is not
- Not a course, a tutorial, or homework with nice wallpaper
- Not another generic AI chatbot
- Not something that interrupts constantly
- Not something that shares anything without permission

## Principles
1. Lives alongside your work, not in a separate tab.
2. Notices what you're doing and responds gently.
3. Explains and guides rather than doing your coursework for you.
4. Private by default. Nothing leaves without your click.
5. Always editable. You can reshape it anytime.
6. Original art and voice only.

## Version 1
- A companion panel with AI chat
- A few useful actions: switch theme, set up a Python project, focus timer,
  explain an error or selected code
- Moments: opening VS Code, getting stuck, coding late, finishing something
- Packs: a default pack plus a private personal layer
- A "Make it yours" page

## Presence
- Home: a panel on the right side, next to the code
- When the panel is closed: a small status bar line that shows its mood
- Inside the code: a small hint next to an error, one click to explain
- Nudges: a small corner card for gentle moments, like late at night
- Speaking up: gentle by default, with quiet, gentle, and chatty settings
- Look: a soft illustrated cat with a few moods
- Details and the first sketch: PRESENCE.md

## Later
- Messaging between people: notes, "ask for help," jumping into Live Share
- A natural-language layer over any installed extension's commands
- Community packs
- Creative corners: art, music, mind labs, stories
